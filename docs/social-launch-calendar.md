# Owner Only Cars: two-week launch calendar

All dates and times below use Central Time. Review links and the live site before each post. Do not claim that the marketplace is scam-proof, fully launched, or processing real transactions until those statements are true.

## Thursday, September 3, 2026 — 10:00 a.m.

**Theme:** What verification does—and does not—mean

Identity verification, vehicle-ownership review, and a vehicle-history report answer three different questions.

- Identity helps establish who is behind an account.
- Ownership review compares the seller and VIN with submitted vehicle documents.
- Vehicle history can surface title brands, odometer information, and certain salvage, total-loss, or theft data.

None of those checks guarantees a vehicle’s condition or a safe transaction. Owner Only Cars is being built to explain the difference clearly.

Read more: https://owneronlycars.com/blog/what-owner-verification-really-means

## Saturday, September 12, 2026 — 10:00 a.m.

**Theme:** Test-drive safety

A test drive should help you evaluate the vehicle—not create unnecessary risk for the buyer or seller.

Meet in daylight, choose a public location, bring another adult when practical, keep personal documents out of the car, and arrange an independent inspection before money changes hands.

Use the checklist: https://owneronlycars.com/blog/private-party-test-drive-safety-checklist

**Theme:** Financing education

Compare written offers for the same amount and term, and ask whether the lender supports private-party purchases.
