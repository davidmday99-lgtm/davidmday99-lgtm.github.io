# Legal review needed

Professional counsel must review before launch:

- Marketplace classification and dealer/broker licensing exposure in every launch jurisdiction.
- State-specific private-sale frequency limits, dealer presumptions, title, odometer, emissions, disclosure, lien, salvage, lemon-law, tax, registration, and temporary-tag rules.
- Terms, privacy notice, consent language, e-signatures, seller attestation, moderation appeals, warranties/disclaimers, limitation of liability, and dispute terms.
- Biometric/privacy requirements for identity verification, including retention/destruction notices and consent.
- FCRA/DPPA or similar implications for vehicle, identity, device, risk, and public-record data.
- FTC Act, state UDAP rules, advertising claims, fee disclosure, auto-renewal (if any), communications, accessibility, and consumer reporting.
- Data retention, deletion, legal hold, breach notification, children/age eligibility, sanctions, subpoena, and law-enforcement response.
- NMVTIS provider agreement, required report disclosures, branding, refresh rules, and permitted use.
- Section 230/content moderation considerations, fraud reporting, record preservation, and moderator safety.

Configurable marketplace rules are policy controls, not legal conclusions. No default threshold should be described as the legal number of vehicles a private person may sell.
